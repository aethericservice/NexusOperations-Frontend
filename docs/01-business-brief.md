# 01. Business Brief

## Executive Summary

OpsPilot AI is an operations intelligence platform for businesses that rely on multiple SaaS tools and need a single source of operational truth. It connects business systems, detects operational issues, explains root cause, and supports action through AI recommendations and automation.

The business is not built as a broad AI platform. It is built as a focused SaaS product designed to solve the real-world problem of fragmented business operations.

## Problem Statement

Most businesses use multiple systems to run the day to day of their business. A single operational issue often spans several tools:

- CRM has stalled customer updates
- Support tickets show unresolved issues
- Finance reports failed or delayed payments
- Inventory systems show stock mismatches
- Slack or email contains urgent alerts
- Sales dashboards show revenue drops

These signals are often disconnected, delayed, or manually assembled. Managers are forced to check multiple systems to understand what happened and what should be done.

## Consequences of the Problem

The lack of integrated operational visibility causes:

- missed revenue opportunities
- delayed customer responses
- unresolved backlog issues
- poor inventory planning
- service-level failures
- recurring customer churn signals
- manual operational firefighting

## Product Solution

OpsPilot AI creates a unified operational layer across SaaS tools. It ingests operational data, identifies anomalies, summarizes the situation, and recommends next actions.

### Example Questions the Product Helps Answer

- Why did sales decrease this week?
- Which customers are at risk?
- Which support issues are unresolved for more than 24 hours?
- Are there delayed payments or failed transactions?
- Is inventory mismatched with actual demand?
- Are our operational SLAs being breached?

## Business Value

The product creates value by helping teams:

- detect issues before they damage revenue
- reduce manual reporting effort
- understand cause and impact faster
- respond to issues with less delay
- automate repetitive follow-up tasks

## Target Market

### Primary segments
- Service businesses with multiple operational tools
- Agencies with client delivery and billing operations
- E-commerce businesses with storefront, payment, CRM, and support data
- Healthcare administration teams with patient service workflows
- Education institutions with admissions, support, and operations data

## Why This Business Can Scale

This product has strong SaaS potential because:

- pain is recurring and frequent
- the product is tied to operational efficiency
- the value is measurable in time and revenue preserved
- integrations create switching cost and lock-in
- data pipelines become more valuable over time

## Current Risks

- Too broad a product scope
- Generic AI positioning instead of problem-specific value
- Weak integration reliability
- Poor alert quality leading to low trust
- Building without clear ROI for a chosen customer segment

## Strategic Recommendation

Launch with a narrow, highly painful use case and a clear ROI narrative.

The best first product direction is:

> AI operations copilot for businesses running multiple SaaS systems, starting with one high-friction niche such as service businesses or e-commerce operations.

## Long-Term Vision

Over time, the platform can expand to:

- more verticals
- more workflow templates
- more integration types
- deeper AI recommendations
- predictive forecasting
- more autonomous orchestration

## Final Positioning Statement

OpsPilot AI helps business owners and operations teams understand what is breaking across their connected business systems and what to do next.

It transforms fragmented operational signals into a single, actionable operations layer.
